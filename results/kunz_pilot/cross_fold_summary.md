# kunz_pilot: across folds

mel_corr vs the cue's TTS (LPCNet resynthesis). Every null scores the SAME output against other cues of its set: **length-matched** = mean over the 10 other cues closest in TTS speech duration; **all cues** = mean over all other cues; **shuffled trial** = one seeded other trial's cue (kept for continuity; lenient, and partly a sentence-length effect). Primary null: **length-matched** (experiment.summary.primary_null), listed first. Excess = own cue - null, paired per output (Wilcoxon p). Identification: rank of the own cue among all cues of the set (1 = best); top-1 rate (chance = mean 1/cues), median rank / cues, p = exact rank-sum test against uniform ranks. Attempted vs imagined: difference of the excesses (Mann-Whitney U, unpaired). Per-fold p values: raw [Holm-adjusted across the folds]. Silent outputs (no speech frame) have no mel_corr and are counted separately. Early speech: decoded speech seconds within the first 4 s of the output. WER: faster-whisper small.en, unconstrained, pooled (null = same transcript vs the shuffled-trial cue). Generated by scripts/summarize_kunz.py.
